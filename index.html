How it works

From your keyboard to a finished video in one step.

1 · Record

Capture straight from your piano

Plug in a digital piano over USB. Falling records every note, velocity and pedal over Web MIDI, and the microphone audio alongside it, on one clock.

2 · Render

Deterministic, fast video

A headless rendering pipeline draws every frame in parallel across CPU cores and encodes to MP4 or ProRes. A 25-second piece renders at 720p60 in about the time it takes to play it.

3 · Share

Synced and ready to post

Audio is aligned to the notes automatically, hands are colored separately, and themes set the look. The result is ready for YouTube, Reels or a lesson.

Built to improve itself

Every render can be rated. Bad results become fixes.

  • Feedback dataset. Users flag a bad render (wrong hand colors, audio drift) and the inputs are captured as a reproducible sample.
  • AI repair agent. An LLM agent, Claude among the supported models, reads the diagnostic report, patches the pipeline and runs the full test suite before anything ships.
  • Guardrail tests. Every fixed defect becomes a permanent regression test, so quality only moves one way.
  • Automated tuning loop. An experimental search loop proposes changes to the audio pipeline in sandboxes, scores them against ground truth, and keeps only the winners.
